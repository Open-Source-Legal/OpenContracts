# Oncor delivery fixtures

These fixtures establish current, historical, and technical-guide record
shapes without inventing an approving PUCT order identifier. The historical
record is the official Revision Twenty tariff, effective November 27, 2017.
Legal review must still establish its complete effective interval and the stable
`puct-order` key approving that version.

Public availability does not establish public-domain status. Source collection
must remain disabled until the reuse policy and provider license declaration are
approved.
