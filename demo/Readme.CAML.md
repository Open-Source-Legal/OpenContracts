>! Section 01
## Overview

This corpus collects the primary registration statements and exhibits of two
2026 IPO filings — **Space Exploration Technologies Corp** (S-1, filed
2026-05-20) and **Fervo Energy Company** (S-1, filed 2026-04-17) — drafted by
the world's top securities law firms. It is the pilot corpus for the
OpenContracts **reference enrichment engine**: a deterministic pass that turns
the citations buried in filing prose into first-class, navigable links.

## The Law Layer

The statutes these filings cite are not footnotes here — they are **documents
in their own corpora**, one per section, carrying the full statutory text from
official sources. Citations resolve across corpus boundaries to:

:::: cards {columns: 2}
- **Delaware General Corporation Law** | 10 sections | #f5b942
  Title 8, Chapter 1 — the corporate-law backbone. Sections 116, 122, 145, 151,
  202, 203, 212, 224, 228, and 242, fetched from the official Delaware Code.
- **Securities Act of 1933** | 6 sections | #ffd166
  Sections 2, 4, 7, 8, 11, and 22 — registration, exemptions, and liability for
  the offering itself.
- **Securities Exchange Act of 1934** | 5 sections | #f4a261
  Sections 10, 12, 13, 14, and 16. Section 16 (insider reporting and
  short-swing profits) is the single most-cited provision in the corpus, with
  21 separate citations across both filings.
- **Internal Revenue Code** | 4 sections | #e9c46a
  Sections 368, 451, 501, and 1202 — reorganizations, income timing, and
  qualified small business stock.

::: chapter {#how}
>! Section 04
## How This Was Built

The web is generated, not curated. The enrichment engine extracts references
with high-precision grammars, resolves them against the corpus (and against
every visible authority corpus), and persists the result as ordinary
OpenContracts objects — span annotations for every mention, document
relationships for the document graph, and cross-corpus reference records keyed
by canonical citation (dgcl:145, exchange-act:16). Re-running the pass is
idempotent: as new filings or new bodies of law are added, only the new
references appear.
