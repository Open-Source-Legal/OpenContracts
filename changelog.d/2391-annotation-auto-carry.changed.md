- Carry human annotations and their relationships onto each new document version automatically, flagged until reviewed (`opencontractserver/annotations/services/version_review.py::carry_version`, queued from `tasks/doc_tasks.py::set_doc_lock_state` once parsing finishes). Unique exact matches become `AUTO` successors, the rest `STALE`; both count as needing review until a person approves, corrects or drops them. Previously nothing was carried and every annotation had to be re-created by hand.
- `AnnotationVersionDecision` is now one row per annotation (`OneToOne`) with `AUTO`/`STALE` states, a nullable `reviewer` and `reviewed_at`; migration `annotations/0106` (unreleased) is amended in place. `STALE` rows move to the next version and are re-matched instead of being stranded two versions back; a version that finishes parsing after its successor re-runs that successor's carry, so out-of-order parses lose nothing.
- Human relationships are copied once every endpoint resolves onto the new version through the successor chain, and pruned when a dropped successor leaves them empty.
- GraphQL: `AnnotationVersionState` gains `AUTO`; `AnnotationType.versionState` reports how a current-version annotation arrived (auto-carried vs approved/corrected); `DocumentType.staleAnnotationCount` is renamed `annotationsNeedingReview` and counts both pending states; `AnnotationVersionReview.proposedPlacement` is removed (the auto-carried successor is the proposal).
- Frontend: shared `VersionStateBadge` highlights pending annotations in the sidebar, review panel and version pill; dropping a carried annotation removes it and its emptied relationships from the viewer.
