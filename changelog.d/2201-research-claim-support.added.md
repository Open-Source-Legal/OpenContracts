- **Deep research: finalize verifies that a cited passage SUPPORTS its sentence, and citing is now cheap** (closes #2201). `_verify_cite_spans` (`opencontractserver/research/services/research_reports.py`) generalizes the #2189 quote verifier from "is this quote verbatim" to "does the source say this": every cited sentence must share at least `RESEARCH_CLAIM_SUPPORT_MIN_COVERAGE` (0.25) of its content words with the text of the annotation(s) it cites, or the citation is stripped (the prose survives as uncited analysis) and `report.warnings` records it. The check is a deterministic lexical floor — no LLM call or embedding round-trip at finalize — and decisively kills the two anchoring failures from #2201: a one-word mention span cited for a full sentence, and prompt-derived background decorated with a loosely-related entity anchor. Claims under `RESEARCH_CLAIM_SUPPORT_MIN_WORDS` (12) words are accepted unchecked, since a fragment has too few content words for a ratio to mean anything; `_claim_is_supported` is the single seam to swap in an entailment call later.
- **Deep research: `find_citable_passages` replaces `search_exact_text_as_sources` in the agent's toolset.** The prompt's #2180 rule told the agent to pull a pinpoint anchor with `search_exact_text_as_sources` and cite it — impossible, because that tool returns synthetic *negative* annotation ids that `record_finding` rejects and `finalize` drops. The agent burned a 3M-token run hunting for an id it could never obtain. The new closure (`opencontractserver/tasks/research_tasks.py`) is backed by `AnnotationService.search_corpus_annotation_text` (`opencontractserver/annotations/services/annotation_service.py`): it returns the real corpus annotations containing a phrase, tightest (most pinpoint) first, each with a ready-to-paste `cite` handle, and registers them in the run's citation accumulator. Visibility is delegated wholesale to `get_corpus_annotations`, so it adds no permission logic of its own. `search_exact_text_as_sources` is dropped from `DEEP_RESEARCH_READ_ONLY_TOOLS`.
