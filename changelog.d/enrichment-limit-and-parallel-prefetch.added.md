- `CustomsRulingCitationService.enrich_corpus()` / `enrich_customs_rulings` management command: added an optional `limit` (`--limit N`) to scan a deterministic (lowest-id-first) subset of a corpus instead of the whole thing — useful for a quick, fully-complete pass over a manageable slice (e.g. evaluating enrichment output quality and UX) on a corpus too large to enrich end-to-end in one sitting. Citation resolution still considers every document's title in the corpus, not just the scanned subset, so a limited run can still resolve citations to sibling rulings outside the slice.
- `CustomsRulingCitationService.enrich_corpus()`: parallelized the per-document `load_document_text_and_layer` call (S3 fetch + PAWLS JSON parse, ~230ms/doc measured, dominated by I/O not the regex matching that follows) across a 12-worker thread pool; DB writes stay single-threaded on the caller. That call touches no ORM state, so it's safe to prefetch across threads.
