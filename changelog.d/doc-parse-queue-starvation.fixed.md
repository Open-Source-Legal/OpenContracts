- `config/settings/base.py`: routed `extract_thumbnail`, `ingest_doc`, and `set_doc_lock_state` to a new dedicated `doc_parse` Celery queue, separate from `convert_document_to_pdf` (still on the default `celery` queue). Previously all four stages of the per-document ingest chain shared one queue; on a `.doc`-heavy bulk corpus, conversion tasks for every document get enqueued up front while a document's later-stage tasks only appear once its own conversion finishes, so the flood of cheap conversion tasks statistically starved the actual parsing/embedding work — observed on a 220K-document bulk ingest at a ~190:1 completion ratio (`convert_document_to_pdf` vs. `ingest_doc`) even after doubling worker concurrency. `compose/local/django/celery/worker/start` and `compose/production/django/celery/worker/start` updated to subscribe to `doc_parse` alongside `celery`/`worker_uploads` — required so standard (non-bulk) document uploads keep processing under the new routing instead of stalling on an unconsumed queue. Ops docs updated to match the new routing: `docs/upload_methods/worker_celery_setup.md` (queue table, TL;DR worker command, verification and symptom sections now cover `doc_parse`), plus the `-Q` queue lists in `worker_uploads.md`, `remote_ingest_worker.md`, and `upload_methods/index.md` — the previous text told self-hosted operators that `-Q celery,worker_uploads` was sufficient, which under the new routing would leave every document silently stuck mid-chain with nothing consuming `doc_parse`.
