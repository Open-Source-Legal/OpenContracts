- `CustomsRulingCitationService.enrich_corpus()` (`opencontractserver/enrichment/services/customs_ruling_citation_service.py`): citation resolution built its ruling-number → document `title_index` from the raw, uppercased document title. The citation regex only ever extracts the bare ruling number (e.g. `A83482`), but ~98% of a real CROSS-style corpus's ingested titles retain their materialized filename extension (`A83482.doc`), so the index key never matched and every cross-document citation into those documents silently resolved as unresolved — `citations_resolved: 0` regardless of `citation_candidates` found. Added `_ruling_number_from_title()` (uses `pathlib.Path(...).stem` to strip at most one trailing extension) and applied it to both `title_index` construction and each document's own `own_number` lookup.
